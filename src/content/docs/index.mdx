		<h2>About</h2>
		<p>{'Modern robot policies are often presented as a model and a benchmark score. On a physical robot, the result also depends on the data collection setup, robot interface, training pipeline, inference loop, and evaluation protocol. We will study these pieces together using the SO-101 arm and the LeRobot software stack.'}</p>
		<p>{'The course begins with assembly and teleoperation, moves through single-task imitation learning, and then studies generalist robot policies and current research directions. Most assignments will be completed in small teams. There are no exams.'}</p>

	<section id="faq" className="course-section course-faq">
		<h2>FAQ</h2>

		<h3>Who should take this course?</h3>
		<p>{'The course is intended for students who are comfortable with Python and basic machine-learning workflows and who want experience with physical robot systems. Prior robotics experience is useful, but not required.'}</p>

		<h3>Do students need to buy a robot?</h3>
		<p>{'No. The course will provide shared SO-101 arms and related hardware for team use.'}</p>

		<h3>How are teams graded?</h3>
		<p>{'Assignments are primarily team submissions, but team grades are not automatically copied to every member. Individual understanding and documented contribution are part of the remaining 20% of the grade.'}</p>

		<h3>Are there exams?</h3>
		<p>{'No. Evaluation is based on the assignments, participation, team contribution, and individual assessment.'}</p>

		<h3>How much work happens outside class?</h3>
		<p>{'Teams should expect regular work outside the weekly meeting, especially for data collection, training, and robot evaluation. Hardware access and scheduling details will be shared at the beginning of the semester.'}</p>

		<h3>Can students use coding assistants?</h3>
		<p>{'Yes, for explanation, debugging, refactoring, and documentation. Students remain responsible for submitted code and results, must disclose material use, and must be able to explain their work. Fabricated logs, videos, experiments, or results are not permitted.'}</p>

		<h3>What happens when hardware fails?</h3>
		<p>{'Report the problem early and document what happened. Ordinary hardware failures are part of working with robots; unsafe operation or unreported damage is a separate issue.'}</p>
