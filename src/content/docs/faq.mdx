title: FAQ
description: Common questions about the course.
---

## Do I need prior robotics experience?

Some robotics experience is preferred, but the most important requirement is comfort with ML/deep-learning workflows. We will review needed robotics concepts, but this is not an introductory robotics class.

## Do I need my own robot?

No. The course uses department-sponsored SO-101 follower-arm kits shared across teams.

## How many students will be admitted?

Recommended first-offering cap is 30 students.

## How are teams formed?

Structured self-selection with instructor balancing.

## Will there be exams?

No.

## Is there a separate graduate project?

No, not in the first offering.

## What software stack will we use?

Hugging Face LeRobot with SO-101 robot arms.

## Do we need two cameras?

Fixed external camera is the canonical minimum; wrist camera is strongly preferred for VLA-style work.

## What if my team’s robot breaks?

Report quickly; spare kits/parts and repair tokens exist.

## What if cloud compute fails?

Reduced configs, starter datasets, starter checkpoints, and fallback options will be provided.

## Can we use ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Codex, or other coding assistants?

Yes, with disclosure.

## How much does leaderboard rank matter?

It is motivational, not the main basis for grades.

## Can my team choose a different final task?

Yes for Assignment 4, subject to approval and safety constraints.
