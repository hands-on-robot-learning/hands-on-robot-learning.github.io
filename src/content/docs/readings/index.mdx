Reading lists and links are **coming soon** and will be updated as the semester approaches.

## Paper/system dissection questions

1. What is the robot?
2. What is the observation?
3. What is the action space?
4. What data was used?
5. What supervision signal was used?
6. What part is learned vs. engineered?
7. What breaks?
8. Could we reproduce a toy version on SO-101?
9. What would the leaderboard metric be?
10. What is the one idea worth stealing?

## Guest speakers

Guest speakers will be encouraged to discuss what failed and what was hard to debug, not only polished results.
