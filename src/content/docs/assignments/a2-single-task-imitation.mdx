A2 covers the complete single-task imitation-learning loop: define a task, collect demonstrations, train a policy, deploy it on the SO-101, and evaluate the resulting behavior.

## Tasks

- Write down the task, reset distribution, observations, actions, and success criterion.
- Collect and inspect a real-robot demonstration dataset.
- Train an action-chunking, diffusion, or flow-based policy using the course stack.
- Deploy the policy with a fixed inference and control configuration.
- Evaluate it under the assigned protocol and compare it with your A1 system where the tasks overlap.

## Submission

- Dataset and a short dataset card describing collection, filtering, and known problems.
- Training configuration, logs, and the checkpoint used for evaluation.
- Deployment and evaluation code or commands.
- Results from every required evaluation trial, with continuous video.
- A short report connecting data and inference choices to observed behavior.

## Assessment

The submission will be graded on the quality of the data and implementation, reproducibility of the training and deployment path, consistency of the evaluation, and the quality of the analysis. Report all assigned trials, not only successful ones.
