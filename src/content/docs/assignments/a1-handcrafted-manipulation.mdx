**Weight:** 10%  
**Format:** Team assignment  
**Due:** To be announced

Before training a policy, your team will implement one manipulation skill with an explicit pipeline. The point is to understand what can be solved with calibration, perception, geometry, and control—and where those pieces fail.

## Scope

The task and common evaluation setup will be specified when the assignment is released. Possible components include color or marker-based perception, a calibrated camera-to-robot transform, scripted waypoints, visual servoing, and a small library of motion primitives.

The final behavior should not be an end-to-end learned policy. A learned detector or segmenter may be used as one component if its role is clearly described.

## Submission

- Source code and configuration.
- A short description of the perception and control pipeline, including its assumptions.
- Results from the required evaluation trials, with the success criterion stated before reporting results.
- Continuous video covering representative successes and failures.
- A brief failure analysis and a list of changes your team tried.

## Assessment

We will consider whether the system is correctly implemented, whether the evaluation is consistent, and whether the analysis follows from the evidence. A result that works once is not enough to characterize a robot skill.
