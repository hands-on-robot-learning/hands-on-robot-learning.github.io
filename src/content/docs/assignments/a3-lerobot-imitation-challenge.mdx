## Goal

Collect real robot data, train an ACT or diffusion-policy-style model, deploy it on the SO-101 arm, and evaluate on the official cube pick-and-place benchmark.

## What you will learn

- What makes real-robot datasets trainable (and what breaks training)
- How to run a reproducible training loop (configs, checkpoints, logs)
- How to evaluate fairly on hardware with continuous video evidence

## Required hardware/software

- SO-101 follower arm
- Fixed external camera (minimum)
- LeRobot tooling for datasets + training

## Dataset target

- Baseline: Approximately 50 valid episodes
- Stronger attempt: 100+ valid episodes if leader-arm scheduling and hardware reliability permit
- Disclosure: Teams must disclose whether they used class starter data, their own data, or both.

## Evaluation protocol (official)

- Run **10 official evaluation trials** under the assigned protocol.
- Record **continuous unedited video** for official runs.
- No human intervention during trials.
- Report all required trials, not only successes.

## Common failure modes

<aside className="hol-assignment-callout hol-assignment-callout--failure">
	<h3>Common failure modes</h3>
	<ul>
		<li>“Looks good in validation, fails on robot” due to mismatch in control frequency / latency / camera setup</li>
		<li>Dataset issues: inconsistent resets, mislabeled episodes, silent logging bugs</li>
		<li>Compounding error: policy drifts into unrecoverable states after a few steps</li>
		<li>Overfitting to a narrow scene distribution</li>
	</ul>
</aside>

## How to get unstuck

<aside className="hol-assignment-callout hol-assignment-callout--tip">
	<h3>Debugging tips</h3>
	<ul>
		<li>Overfit a tiny subset on purpose to verify the pipeline end-to-end (then scale up).</li>
		<li>Compare against A2 baseline with the same evaluation harness and videos.</li>
		<li>When debugging, change one thing at a time and keep a short experiment log.</li>
	</ul>
</aside>

## Extension ideas

- Low-data track: improve performance with fewer episodes (with clear reporting)
- Robustness: lighting changes, cube pose variations, mild camera perturbations
- Compare ACT vs diffusion under identical protocol and dataset
