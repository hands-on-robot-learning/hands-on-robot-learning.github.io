A0 is a hardware bring-up check. Your team will assemble an SO-101 arm, configure its motors, calibrate its joints, and verify that the resulting setup behaves as expected.

## Tasks

- Assemble the arm and inspect the mechanical and electrical connections.
- Assign and verify motor IDs.
- Calibrate each joint and save the resulting configuration.
- Define a safe home pose and conservative motion limits.
- Check the camera and the course software environment.
- Run a short motion test and stop if the robot behaves unexpectedly.

## Submission

- Calibration and configuration files.
- A short continuous video showing the arm moving from its home pose through the requested test motion.
- Brief setup notes: hardware changes, calibration problems, repairs, and anything the course staff should know.

## Assessment

We will check that the arm is assembled correctly, moves safely, and can be reproduced from the submitted files and notes. A robot that required troubleshooting is not a weaker submission; undocumented troubleshooting is.

## Safety

Keep hands and loose objects outside the workspace while the robot is moving. Use conservative speeds for the first test, power down before mechanical adjustments, and report damaged hardware promptly.
