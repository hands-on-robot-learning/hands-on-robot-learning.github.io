import { parseYaml } from '../../lib/yaml';
import scheduleYaml from '../../data/schedule.yaml?raw';

export const weeks = (parseYaml(scheduleYaml).weeks ?? []);

This schedule is **tentative** and may change based on hardware availability and how fast we collectively debug the stack.

Most weeks start with a **Robot Mystery of the Week**: a short failure case to diagnose (perception vs calibration vs control vs policy).

## Major assignment rhythm

- **A1** released Week 2, due Week 3
- **A2** released Week 4, due Week 6
- **A3** released Week 6, due Week 10
- **A4** released Week 10, due Week 15

No major deadlines are scheduled during **fall break** or **fall recess** (see notes in the week-by-week plan).
