## Goals

- Your arm homes reliably and repeatably
- Your camera is mounted and stable
- You can record a short teleop episode end-to-end

## Checklist

- Arm assembled with consistent cable routing
- Calibration saved and versioned
- Teleop command latency tested (subjective notes are OK)
