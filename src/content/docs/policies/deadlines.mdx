title: Deadlines and Repair Tokens
description: Target deadlines, grace windows, and what to do when hardware breaks.
---

Deadlines in this course are **targets**. We want teams to iterate weekly and keep experiments reproducible, not panic at the last minute.

## Target deadlines and Monday grace windows

When a deadline is listed (often on a Friday), there is typically a short **Monday grace window** for finishing up and packaging work cleanly.

If you are slipping, communicate early and include evidence of progress.

## Friday status updates

A Friday status update should include:

- What is complete
- What is still failing
- What evidence the team has
- What the team will try next
- Whether the team needs hardware, compute, or instructor help

## Repair tokens

Each team has **two repair tokens**.

- Repair tokens can be used for genuine hardware, camera, leader-arm, cloud/GPU, or infrastructure failures.
- They are not meant for ordinary procrastination.

## Breaks and recess

There are **no major deadlines** during fall break or fall recess.

## Late work and communication

Late work without communication may lose credit.
