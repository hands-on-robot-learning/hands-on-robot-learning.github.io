---
title: LLM / coding-assistant policy
description: Permissive use with attribution, understanding, and responsibility.
---

Students may use LLMs/coding assistants such as **ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Codex**, and similar systems.

<div className="hol-llm-grid">
	<article>
		<h2>Allowed</h2>
		<ul>
			<li>Explaining concepts</li>
			<li>Debugging errors</li>
			<li>Generating starter code</li>
			<li>Refactoring code</li>
			<li>Writing documentation</li>
			<li>Drafting experiment plans</li>
			<li>Brainstorming failure hypotheses</li>
			<li>Implementing code-as-policies-style robot programs</li>
			<li>Improving clarity of reports</li>
		</ul>
	</article>
	<article>
		<h2>Required</h2>
		<ul>
			<li>Disclose the tool used.</li>
			<li>Briefly describe how it was used.</li>
			<li>Test and understand generated code.</li>
			<li>Cite external code, models, or datasets suggested by the tool.</li>
			<li>Avoid uploading private, sensitive, or privileged information.</li>
		</ul>
	</article>
	<article className="hol-llm-card--warning">
		<h2>Not allowed</h2>
		<ul>
			<li>Fabricating results</li>
			<li>Inventing numbers</li>
			<li>Hiding manual interventions</li>
			<li>Generating fake logs or videos</li>
			<li>Submitting code the team cannot explain</li>
			<li>Misrepresenting contribution</li>
			<li>Uploading private or PII data</li>
		</ul>
	</article>
</div>

<aside className="hol-policy-callout">
	<h2>Use the tools, own the work</h2>
	<p>
		{
			'Teams are responsible for everything they submit. Disclosure is not a penalty; it is part of reproducible engineering and honest collaboration.'
		}
	</p>
</aside>
