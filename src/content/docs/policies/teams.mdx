## Team size and enrollment

- Target team size: **3 students**
- Teams of **2–4** may be allowed
- Recommended first-offering cap: **30 students**

## Team formation process

1. Survey
2. In-class mixer
3. Students propose teams
4. Instructor finalizes teams to balance skill sets, schedules, and hardware capacity

## Contribution statements

Assignments may require a short contribution statement describing who did what (hardware, data, training, evaluation, writing).

## Peer evaluation

We will use mid-semester and final peer evaluations to help teams reflect and to ensure contributions are recognized.

## Individual credit assignment

- Default: the same team grade is applied to all team members.
- The instructor may adjust individual credit for severe non-participation or misconduct **after warning and a chance to repair when appropriate**.
