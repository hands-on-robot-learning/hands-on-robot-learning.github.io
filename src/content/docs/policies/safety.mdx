title: Safety
description: Safe operation rules for the SO-101 hardware and lab space.
---

SO-101 is a small robot arm, but it is still a **physical robot**. Treat it like lab equipment.

## Safety rules

- Keep hands clear during autonomous execution
- Use safe home poses and velocity limits
- Keep workspaces clear
- Stop immediately if robot behaves unexpectedly
- Report damaged hardware promptly
- Do not bypass safety limits for leaderboard performance
- Power down before mechanical adjustments
- Check cables before motion scripts
- Keep liquids away

## Autonomous execution checklist

- Workspace clear (no loose tools, cables, or clutter)
- Camera(s) connected and stable
- Correct robot configuration loaded
- Safe home pose defined and reachable
- Velocity limits set conservatively
- Emergency stop plan ready (know how you will stop motion)
- First run at low speed with a short horizon

## Damaged hardware

Report damaged hardware quickly. Ordinary failures that are honestly reported are not punished.

## Unsafe operation

Unsafe operation may reduce credit or result in loss of hardware access.
